============================================================ Use a modern sans-serif. Preferred: Inter Geist Manrope Plus Jakarta Sans Use strong hierarchy. Suggested desktop: Display: 56–72px H1: 40–52px H2: 28–36px H3: 20–26px Body: 15–18px Metadata: 12–14px Mobile: Display: 38–48px H1: 30–38px H2: 24–30px H3: 18–22px Body: 15–17px Do not make mobile typography tiny. Use font weight, spacing and hierarchy rather than decorative effects.
